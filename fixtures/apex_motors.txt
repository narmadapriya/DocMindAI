2. FY2025 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
======================================================================

Apex Motors closed fiscal year 2025 with $612.4 million in revenue on
16,070 vehicles sold, marking the company's strongest year since its 2015
founding.

Growth accelerated each quarter. Q4 contributed $181.1 million in revenue
and 4,610 units, representing a 34% increase in units compared with Q1.

Performance was driven by:
- Strong demand for the Apex SUV Trailblazer.
- Continued expansion in the South region.
- Steady gains in average transaction price.
- A product mix shift toward higher-margin SUV and truck models.

Customer satisfaction (CSAT): 4.6 out of 5.

9. MARKET ANALYSIS
======================================================================

The market report states that the North American light-vehicle market grew at
a mid-single-digit pace in 2025, while Apex Motors significantly outpaced the
industry. Over the 2021-2025 period, Apex's sales index nearly doubled, while
the broader industry's increase was less than half that rate.

Key market drivers identified in the report:
- Continued consumer shift toward SUVs and crossovers, supporting the
  Trailblazer.
- Gradual but accelerating EV adoption, supporting double-digit growth in
  Volt-X reservations.
- Regional population growth in the South, reinforcing Apex's largest
  existing dealer network.
- Rising average transaction prices industry-wide, consistent with Apex's
  FY2025 shift toward higher-margin models.

11. FY2026 OUTLOOK
======================================================================

Management is targeting 12% unit sales growth for FY2026.

Planned priorities include:
- A 3,000-unit increase in Apex EV Volt-X production capacity.
- Opening two new regional service centers.
- Expansion in the East region.
- Continued customer-experience investment aimed at moving CSAT from 4.6
  toward a target of 5.0.
- Disciplined cost management across manufacturing and supply-chain
  operations.
